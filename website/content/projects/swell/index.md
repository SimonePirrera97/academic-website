SWELL develops smart control technology for wave energy converters, with the goal of reducing the levelized cost of energy and supporting the commercialization of wave energy.

The project combines nonlinear hydrodynamic modeling, data-driven model predictive control, convex relaxation techniques, experimental validation, and open-source software. It is coordinated by Simone Pirrera with supervision from Diego Regruto Tomalino, Hugh Wolgamot, and David Angeli.

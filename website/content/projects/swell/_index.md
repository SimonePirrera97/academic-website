    <h2>Smart Wave Energy Conversion via Learning and Low-Cost Control</h2>
    <p>SWELL develops data-driven model predictive control for wave energy converters, with the goal of improving real-time performance while keeping sensing and actuation requirements practical.</p>
    <p>The project combines nonlinear hydrodynamic and data-driven modeling, efficient MPC design, experimental validation, and open-source software to help make wave energy more competitive.</p>
    <p>This MSCA project is part of the Horizon Europe programme, 2025 call. The grant agreement has been signed under project number 101276493, and SWELL started on June 16, 2026.</p>
    <p>The total funding assigned to the project is about €332K over 36 months: 16 months at UWA, 5 months at Imperial College London, and the remaining 15 months at the host institution, Politecnico di Torino.</p>
